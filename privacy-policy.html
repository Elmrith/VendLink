Privacy Policy

VendLink is a food sourcing and ordering platform that helps vendors and suppliers manage product availability, purchasing, and communication. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

1. Information we collect

We collect the personal and business information needed to operate the marketplace and protect the platform. This may include:

  • Name, email address, and contact details
  • Business name, address, municipality, province, barangay, and payment information required for order settlement
  • Profile images, business permit images, and account verification details
  • Order information, delivery details, and account activity
  • Consent records, cookie choices, and optional marketing preferences

2. Why we collect information

VendLink uses information to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts
  • Review and approve vendor or supplier applications
  • Facilitate orders, delivery, and payment communication
  • Send security, verification, and transactional notifications
  • Improve service quality and prevent fraud
  • Provide lawful access to customer support and privacy requests

3. How information is stored and protected

Personal information is stored in the VendLink MySQL database or restricted file storage on controlled servers and protected with access controls, hashed passwords, session security measures, and careful operational safeguards. Business permit images are available only to authorized administrators for application review. We retain data only as long as necessary for legitimate business and legal obligations.

4. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with trusted service providers that support email delivery, authentication, storage, and customer support. Each third-party service is reviewed for relevance and necessity. We never share personal information with third parties without a valid purpose and appropriate legal basis.

  • PHPMailer and Brevo SMTP are used only for account and transactional email delivery.
  • Lucide Icons and other front-end assets are used to render interface design and accessibility cues.
  • Analytics or advertising tools, if added later, will be gated behind cookie consent.

5. Cookies and consent

VendLink uses essential cookies to keep the site secure and your session active. Non-essential cookies are only used after you provide explicit consent. You can change your consent at any time from the Cookie Settings section or the cookie banner.

We do not treat continued website browsing as automatic consent.

6. Your rights

You may request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate information, deletion of eligible data, or withdrawal of consent for optional marketing activities. Where required, we will review your request and respond within a reasonable timeframe. Certain records may be retained to support legitimate business, legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations.

7. Data retention and deletion

VendLink keeps information only while needed for account operation, order processing, recordkeeping, compliance, and security. When a user requests deletion, the request is reviewed and eligible records are removed or anonymized while preserving required records that must legally be retained.

8. Contact and support

For privacy, consent, account access, or deletion requests, contact the VendLink support team at support@vendlink.com. Business information is maintained in the platform settings and available from the Business Information section.
