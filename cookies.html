Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how VendLink uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the site secure, remember preferences, and support the marketplace.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored in a browser to remember information such as login state, preferences, and selected settings.

2. Essential cookies

We use essential cookies to keep sessions secure and to enable the account login experience. These cookies are necessary for the core operation of VendLink and do not require separate consent.

3. Non-essential cookies

Analytics, functional, and marketing cookies may be used when the user has clearly and explicitly opted in. These cookies are not activated until consent is granted, and users may withdraw consent at any time.

4. Your choices

When visiting VendLink for the first time, you are presented with a consent banner with three choices: Accept All, Reject Non-Essential, and Customize Preferences. You may also update your preference from Cookie Settings later.

5. Managing preferences

To change your cookie choices, use the Cookie Settings link in the site footer or return to the cookie banner on a later visit. We do not use non-essential cookies before valid consent is obtained.
